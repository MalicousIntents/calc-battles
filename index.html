No more free points

Sep 8

People were typing the target number and slapping +0 or ×1 on it to "solve" instantly. That's closed now — every submission needs an actual operation on different numbers, checked equation by equation, not just the final answer.

Overflow moved to a fixed time

Sep 6

Overflow now always hits at the 2-minute mark instead of a random window. Also fixed the purple flash sticking around after the round already ended.
